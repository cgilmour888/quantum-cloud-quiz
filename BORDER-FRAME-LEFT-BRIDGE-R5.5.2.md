# BorderFrameEngine R5.5.2 — Left Bridge and Activation Decay

## Scope

R5.5.2 is the final narrow BorderFrameEngine correction before handoff. It restores the artwork-native purple connector immediately left of the placard and removes the persistent trim-overlay artifact that remained while the temporary business card was open.

## Geometry authority

- The immutable 3840 × 2160 MASTER remains the source of all restored purple pixels.
- No broad fill polygon or invented conduit is introduced.
- The R5.5.1 placard-face and ring/Star/altar exclusions remain authoritative.
- The normal product and both true-purple diagnostic modes share the same R5.5.2 mask and phase textures.

## Motion correction

A dedicated purple phase texture supplies continuous counter-clockwise transport through the restored left connector and the original placard frame. Cyan and orange retain the previously approved shared phase data unchanged.

## Activation correction

The placard trim overlay is illuminated only by hover or keyboard focus. Card-open state no longer holds the trim line on screen. `PLACARD_ACTIVATED` continues to trigger a bounded BorderFrameEngine surge with attack, hold, and decay.

## Acceptance boundary

R5.5.2 is not complete until the user approves isolated geometry, moving current, tracer continuity, activation decay, and the normal integrated scene through screenshots or short videos.
