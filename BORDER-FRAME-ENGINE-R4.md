# BorderFrameEngine R4 — Registered Plasma Conduit Network

R4 replaces procedural line geometry and separately positioned border overlays with two full-frame registered texture atlases sampled inside the unified MASTER compositor.

## Locked behavior

- Cyan travels clockwise.
- Orange travels counter-clockwise.
- Purple carries two lower-frequency counter-propagating streams.
- Voltage, packet width, junction amplification, and gameplay impulses vary independently by channel.
- The four gyroscopes, CARL GILMOUR placard, music icon, dashboards, tablet, altar, cloud field, and interior reflections are excluded.
- The route phase remains logically continuous behind the placard while visible emission and bloom remain zero there.
- The placard remains reserved for the independent living-business-card button system.

## Performance target

The default compositor DPR is capped at 1.25. Border shader quality begins at `balanced`, reduces quickly under sustained frame pressure, and recovers slowly. Reduced-motion mode locks the conservative tier.
