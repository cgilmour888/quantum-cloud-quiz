# BorderFrameEngine R5.5 — Production Promotion and Independent Placard

## Geometry authority

The lower-most continuous purple trim physically outlining the CARL GILMOUR
placard is the sole artwork-native authority for the placard geometry. Stage
center and baked text spacing are not used to place the placard hit zone,
exclusion, focus proof, or interaction outline.

## Production promotion

The normal application defaults to cyan, orange, and purple. Production purple
samples `purple-border-production-r5.5.png` and uses the same approved
counter-clockwise cyan-phase transport that passed the isolated true-purple
diagnostic. The legacy `emissive.b * data.a` path is not used for normal purple.

## Independent placard

The physical placard and inner face contain zero production-purple mask pixels.
A transparent polygonal button derived from the traced trim opens Carl Gilmour's
business card on the tablet. The quiz controller remains mounted, so the
question, selection, timer, score, attempts, and streak survive open and close.

## Acceptance boundary

R5.5 is not a final handoff until the user approves screenshots/video of:

1. trim registration over MASTER;
2. normal `/` route with all three animated channels;
3. placard hover/focus/activation;
4. business card on the tablet and quiz-state restoration;
5. protected dashboards, tablet frame, gyroscopes, music icon, and placard face.
