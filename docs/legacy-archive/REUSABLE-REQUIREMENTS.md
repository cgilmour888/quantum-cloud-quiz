# Reusable Product Requirements

The following requirements may be carried into the native rebuild after formal review:

## Exam engine

- ordered and randomized sessions;
- single-select, multiple-select, and select-three support;
- five-option questions with no lost or hidden option;
- clear submission requirements;
- grading and explanations;
- progression, pause, resume, and completion;
- timed and untimed modes;
- bookmarks, missed-question review, and session history.

## Study intelligence

- score and accuracy;
- current and best streak;
- time played and average response time;
- questions answered;
- topic-level performance;
- weakest-topic detection;
- targeted review queues;
- transparent exam-readiness estimate;
- data export and reset.

## Interface

- desktop-first cinematic command-center composition;
- central quiz console, left navigation, right metrics, and lower status region;
- semantic controls and full keyboard operation;
- selected, pending, correct, incorrect, disabled, active, hover, and focus states remain independent;
- dynamic content is never embedded in artwork;
- reduced-motion, reduced-effects, and audio-muted modes.

## Deployment and continuity

- React, TypeScript, and Vite;
- GitHub source control;
- Cloudflare preview before production cutover;
- milestone recovery archives and tested restoration;
- rational asset naming and provenance tracking.
